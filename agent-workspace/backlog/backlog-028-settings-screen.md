## What

One place for what can be chosen: sounds and notifications, cursor style, padding, copy on select, and what later entries add.

## Why

Settings are palette actions today, which works while there are only a few.

## Builds on

Tono takes its theme and font from the desktop, so it needs far fewer settings than Rex Rune's.

## Promote when

When there are enough settings that the palette stops being a good home for them.
