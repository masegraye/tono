## What

An icon for the program in each tab, from Rex's own set.

## Why

The Mac app and Rex Rune both show them, and they make a long sidebar quicker to read.

## Builds on

Tono already knows each tab's foreground program. Rex Rune maps about 150 programs to Rex's icons (`src/app/program_icons/catalog.rs`).

## Promote when

When the sidebar is next worked on.

## Not known yet

Whether Rex's icons may be used here, and how the Mac app's look differs from Rex Rune's.
